The Globe Equity Decree of 1935

A federal law that puts Kearny last in line for every drop. In 2026, Kearny's allotment was cut from 610 acre-feet down to just 0.76. It's a legal straightjacket that treats the town as a "junior" priority.

Chapter V — The Quest for Independence

Understanding the Two Aquifers

Why shallow wells are failing — and why going deeper changes everything.

Kearny's shallow alluvial aquifers are tied to the river and evaporate quickly in the heat. But beneath them sits fractured granite — deep storage vaults protected from the sun and potentially outside the reach of the 1935 Decree.
