Why I’m Writing

My goal with SaveKearnyAZ.org is to inject transparent, high-leverage data into our town's problem-solving efforts. The initiative is structured as a collaborative blueprint where the Town of Kearny, forward-thinking private citizens, and major regional corporations can coordinate on a solution that secures the entire basin. According to recent ADWR public records, the infrastructure required to secure Kearny's water independence already exists. Project 88 is a technical verification initiative, not a commercial pitch. Our singular objective right now is logical certainty: verify what is already in the ground before spending a single public dollar.

The Wastewater Situation

To maintain absolute transparency with local residents, we have also launched a community resource page detailing the wastewater treatment plant status. It provides a straightforward breakdown of engineering timelines, repairs, and actionable guidelines for the public, live at savekearnyaz.org/wastewater-update.html.

The Partnership Proposal

The two deep Vault wells located at Sites 23CCC and 23CAA represent an established structural asset that would cost between $500K–$1M+ to replicate today. The capital is already deployed in the ground. What I am proposing is a swift, public-private partnership to fast-track deep-well validation and explore MARS recharge feasibility. Phase 1 requires nothing more than a localized downhole camera survey. It is an ultra-low-cost action step that resolves our single biggest variable instantly: Is the water ready for extraction?

Long-term water stabilization protects every stakeholder in the basin, including the large-scale enterprises operating nearby. To fund this acceleration, I am actively identifying outside partners with firm ESG mandates, water infrastructure groups, and Arizona-based companies eager to support a resilient community. The strategy is designed to inject outside capital and logistical leverage directly into Kearny, with zero extractive friction. Let's schedule a brief, 10-minute introductory call next week to review the ADWR asset profiles together and see if this alignment makes sense for the town.
