Dear Mayor Stacy,

First and foremost, thank you for your continued leadership and transparency during this dual infrastructure crisis. The community’s extraordinary response to your conservation orders—achieving a remarkable 33% reduction—is a direct reflection of the trust you have built with the people of Kearny.

As the developer of SaveKearnyAZ.org and the Project 88 water initiative, my primary goal is to leverage our digital presence to build outside visibility and secure strategic resources. To that end, I would like to propose a specific avenue for the Town’s consideration: a formal private-public partnership with ASARCO and Resolution Copper.

"Teamwork is the fuel that allows common people to attain uncommon results." — Andrew Carnegie —

Both corporations hold senior water rights in the Gila River system, and a substantial portion of their essential workforce calls Kearny home. Because their operational continuity is directly tied to the stability of our municipal grid, we have a compelling case for corporate investment. This is not a request for charity; it is an invitation for an act of workforce preservation and operational self-interest on their behalf.

Our community briefing page at savekearnyaz.org/wastewater-update.html outlines the current wastewater plant situation and the $1.54M USACE Water Infiltration Improvements. Should the Town be open to a collaborative dialogue with corporate executive leadership, I would welcome the opportunity to share specific ideas on how we can present this joint initiative in a manner that perfectly reflects the Town’s values and long-term vision.

Respectfully submitted,
