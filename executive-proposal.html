Why I’m Writing

My goal with SaveKearnyAZ.org is to bring positive, transparent energy to problem solving. The site was built as a template for cooperation — an invitation to develop a collaborative strategy where the Town of Kearny, private citizens, and large corporations with a stake in regional water stability can come together to solve a problem none of them can solve alone. The data I’ve uncovered in ADWR public records suggests the Town may already own the infrastructure to secure its water future. Project 88 is a research initiative, not a sales pitch. The goal is simple: verify what’s in the ground before spending a dollar.

The Wastewater Situation

We’ve also built a dedicated community resource page covering the wastewater treatment plant situation — an honest, transparent breakdown of what happened, what’s being fixed, and what residents need to know. It’s live at savekearnyaz.org/wastewater-update.html.

The Partnership Proposal

What I’d like to explore with you — at your pace, on your terms — is whether a private-public partnership could help Kearny move faster on deep-well verification and MARS recharge feasibility. The two Vault wells at Sites 23CCC and 23CAA represent infrastructure that would cost $500K–$1M+ to replicate from scratch. It’s already in the ground. A downhole camera survey — Phase 1 — costs next to nothing and answers the biggest question immediately: Is the water there?

Stabilizing Kearny’s water resources benefits everyone in the basin — including the corporations already operating here. I’m actively exploring partnerships with entities that have ESG mandates, water technology firms, and Arizona-based companies who understand what it means to invest in a small town fighting for its survival. The goal is to bring resources to Kearny, not extract anything from it.

No rush on any of this, Mayor. I know you’ve got a full plate and then some. Consider this a seed planted. When the timing is right, I’d welcome a conversation — even a short one — about how we might work together to write the next chapter of Kearny’s story.

Thank you for everything you do for that community. It matters more than most people will ever know.

Respectfully,
