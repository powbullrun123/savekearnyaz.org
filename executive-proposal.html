Dear Mayor Stacy,

Thank you for your continued leadership and transparency through this dual infrastructure crisis. The community’s response to your conservation orders — a 33% reduction achieved and held — speaks directly to the trust you have built with the people of Kearny. This town has grit, and that starts at the top.

As the team behind SaveKearnyAZ.org and the Project 88 deep well initiative, we have been working to bring outside visibility and resources to Kearny’s water fight. In that spirit, I want to raise one specific avenue worth the Town’s consideration: a private-public partnership with ASARCO and Resolution Copper.

Both companies hold senior water rights in the Gila River system — but more importantly, a substantial portion of their essential workforce calls Kearny home. Their operational continuity is directly tied to the stability of this municipal grid. That alignment creates a genuine case for corporate investment — not as charity, but as a direct act of workforce preservation and operational self-interest.

“Teamwork is the fuel that allows common people to attain uncommon results.”

— Andrew Carnegie

If we want uncommon results for Kearny, it is time to remind our industrial partners that a town cannot be treated as a one-way resource. True corporate responsibility means investing in the foundation that sustains the enterprise. By inviting these companies to pull on the same rope as our residents, we are not asking for a favor — we are asking them to protect the very ecosystem that makes their business possible.

Should the Town be open to a conversation with their executive leadership, I believe we can frame this as a unified, historic effort to pioneer innovative water solutions in the American Southwest. Project 88 is exactly the kind of initiative that resonates in a boardroom — verifiable geology, a defensible legal framework, federal backing, and a community already doing its part. I would welcome the chance to share specific ideas on how to present this in a way that reflects the Town’s values.

In the meantime, we have built a dedicated community briefing page covering the wastewater plant situation and the $1.54M USACE Water Infiltration Improvements project at:

     savekearnyaz.org/wastewater-update.html

Your feedback would be genuinely welcome — we want it to perfectly mirror the Town’s commitment to transparency as we fight these battles on both fronts.

Thank you for your time, your leadership, and your unwavering dedication to the people of Kearny.

Respectfully,

Powell Parker
