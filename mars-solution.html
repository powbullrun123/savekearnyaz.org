What is MARS?

Managed Aquifer Recharge (MAR) is the standard for the 21st century. By capturing high-quality reclaimed water (effluent) and injecting it directly into deep geological "vaults," we stop the theft of our water by evaporation and industrial drawdown. This isn't just conservation—it's active water banking.

1. CAPTURE

Municipal effluent is harvested from the treatment plant.

2. TREAT

Water is polished to Class A+ standards (cleaner than many rivers).

3. INJECT

Water is "Sleeved" and pumped past the shallow alluvial layer.

4. PROTECT

Stored below 800ft, protected from mining and the sun.

The Chandler Standard

The City of Chandler, Arizona, leads the world in this technology. At the 32-acre Veterans Oasis Park, the city operates five specialized recharge basins. Every drop of water used indoors is recycled.

Using this method, Chandler has banked between 440,000 and 500,000 acre-feet of water underground—creating a "water-savings account" that can be recovered via a network of more than 30 wells during severe surface water shortages.

Proven Success: Beeville, Texas

Kearny isn't alone. Communities like Beeville, Texas, have faced similar drought threats and turned to rehabilitating deep groundwater wells as their primary backup supply. By protecting their deep-well infrastructure, they've secured a future for their residents that surface water alone could never provide.
